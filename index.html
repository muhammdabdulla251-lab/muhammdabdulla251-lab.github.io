About

Who I am

I am Muhammad Abdulla, a founder from Tanzania and a mechatronics engineering student. I design, build and ship my products myself.

My work starts from one question: when a customer goes looking for a business, do they reach it? For a transport company that means knowing its trucks and its costs. For a car rental, a clinic or a school it means a link that opens and a phone that rings.

I work in Swahili and English with clients in any country, and I build for the way customers really reach a business: on the phone and on WhatsApp.
