02 / Studio

AlmindhryBuilds

Email fraud protection for businesses

Fraudsters send fake invoices that look exactly like they came from a company, with new bank details, and its customers pay them. In my November Email Fraud Index, 86% of 537 companies in Oman, the UAE and Tanzania could be faked this way. I close that gap, then keep watch.

  • A free 20-second check of any company's email settings
  • Set up in stages, so the company's own invoices keep arriving
  • Paid only after a public check shows the protection is live
  • A monthly watch for lookalike domains registered to imitate you
See it done on my own email

Also: websites that bring customers. A site that shows the service, the prices and how to book, with call and WhatsApp buttons, the domain in the client's own name, and the Google listing linked to it.
